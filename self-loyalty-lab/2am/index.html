The 2AM Card™

Decide now, while you are calm.

The moment you will need this is the moment your judgment is worst. That is not a weakness in you. It is what wanting someone does to a brain at 2am.

So it gets decided in advance, in daylight, by the version of you who can think.

The wave

If you have stepped back from him, the longing gets louder first, not quieter.

You expect relief. What arrives is a surge of wanting so strong it feels like proof you made a mistake.

It is not proof. It is the wave.

It crests, and it passes, and it passes faster than you think.

Do not decide anything at the peak. Set a timer for twenty minutes.

My three plans

The one person I text instead of him

Tell her she is on this card. Tonight, not later. She will say yes, and then it is real.

From me, to me

Four things that are still true at 2am

A strong bond is proof that you bonded. It is not proof that he was worthy of it.

Missing him is not the same as needing him back. Both can be true at once, and only one of them is a decision.

You are allowed to have needs. Even now. Even after all this time.

You have survived this exact feeling before. Every single time so far.

Tonight, before bed

One line. That's all.

Did I contact him?

That's Day 1. Nothing to change yet.
